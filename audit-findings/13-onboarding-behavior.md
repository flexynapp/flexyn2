# Audit 13 — Onboarding user-visible behavior

Scope: `src/pages/Onboarding.jsx` (12 steps, ~2,888 LOC), supporting helpers
`src/lib/firstLaunch.js`, `src/lib/data/starterRegimen.js`, plus the
post-onboarding nudge layer (`src/components/dashboard/OnboardingNudgeCard.jsx`)
and the nutrition onboarding modal (`src/components/nutrition/NutritionOnboardingModal.jsx`).

Severity: **critical** (blocks signup / data loss), **high** (degraded flow
visibly), **medium** (edge case), **low** (polish).
