# Audit 12 — Gym + Trainer + Corporate user-visible behavior

Scope: GymMap, GymHub, MyGyms, RegisterGym, AdminGyms, AdminReports,
TrainerStudio, TrainerMarket, CorporatePortal, GymEdit plus referenced
gym/trainer components (QrCodeScanner, GymFeedTab, GymLeaderboard,
GymAboutCard, MemberDirectoryModal, GymSignageCard, ListingFormModal).
Scanning for user-visible behavior defects only — migration-level work
is out of scope.

Severity: **H**igh (user-blocking / wrong outcome), **M**edium (degraded
UX or surprising behavior), **L**ow (polish / edge case).

## Notes & meta

- **Out of scope per request**: migration-level findings (already covered in audits 01-07).
- **Most concerning class**: the registration → approval → join chain has multiple "owner is stuck with no recourse" failure modes (#1, #2, #30, #35). A real owner trying to self-register a gym without exact geo coords today gets a pending row that admins can't approve and the owner can't fix.
- **Privacy concern not in table**: every gym feed post + comment leaks email local-part as the visible handle. Combined with non-private corporate-org membership this is a real surface (a CFO posting to a corporate-org feed displays their work email username to everyone).
- **Most user-blocking single bug**: #36 (map search by city is silently broken when the city isn't already in the bbox). Search is a core map affordance and it just appears to not work.
