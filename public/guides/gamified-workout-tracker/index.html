Flexyn

A workout tracker that plays like a game

Streaks and badges get people through the first week. What keeps them training is seeing real progress. Flexyn tries to do both.

Updated September 2026

What it is

Flexyn is a workout tracker built for people who lift. You log sets, it keeps your history and suggests your next weight from it, and an AI coach answers questions about it. On top of that sits a game layer: XP, levels, capsules to open, weekly leagues and crews to train with.

It is a web app. It runs in your browser and installs to your phone's home screen. Native App Store and Play Store apps are in progress but not released yet.

The training side

  • Fast logging. Last session sits next to each set. On barbell lifts, the plates for each side appear under the next set.
  • Next-step suggestions. If no set last session was marked failed it suggests a small jump. If one was, it suggests holding the weight.
  • PR tracking. Sets at 70% of your best or more show how close they are, and personal records are kept per lift.
  • AI coach. Ask about your training in plain language and it answers from what you actually logged. A signed-in account gets 20 AI replies a day, a guest 5.
  • Weekly review. Every Sunday you logged something that week, it summarises it: lifting, cardio, sleep, food and more, depending on what you track.

The game side

  • XP and levels. Training earns XP, and streaks, quests and level-ups pay Flex Coins. The server caps how much of each any account can gain per day.
  • Capsules and a market. Earn capsules by levelling up or buy them with coins, then open them for stickers, titles and frames, and trade them with other lifters.
  • Weekly leagues. Bronze through Legend. Qualifying takes training days, not just logging in.
  • Crews. Train with friends, chase crew challenges for trophies, and take on other crews in week-long Crew Wars.
  • Daily quests and streaks. Small goals for the days you need a reason to show up.

Who it is for

People who train in a gym, want a proper log, and like a bit of competition. If you want a bare spreadsheet, it is probably more than you need. If a tracker has never held your attention past a month, it might be worth a try.

Limits worth knowing

  • No wearable sync yet (Apple Health, Google Fit and Strava are listed as coming soon).
  • Photo meal recognition is capped at 3 scans a day.
  • The app is in English, Spanish and French.

Try it in Flexyn

Flexyn is free to use. Open it in your browser, continue as a guest or sign in with email, and log your first set.
