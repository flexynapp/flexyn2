Plate math for any barbell

Subtract the bar, split what is left in two, then fill each side from the heaviest plate down.

Plate calculator

How the math works

  1. Subtract the bar. A 185 lb target on a 45 lb bar leaves 140 lb of plates.
  2. Halve it. That is 70 lb per side.
  3. Fill each side from the heaviest plate that fits: 45 then 25. Done.

Collars usually weigh little, but competition collars are 2.5 kg each. If you use them, subtract them with the bar.

Standard plates

Pounds: 45, 35, 25, 10, 5, 2.5. Kilograms: 25, 20, 15, 10, 5, 2.5, 1.25. If a target cannot be made exactly with the plates you have, the calculator shows the closest weight below it.
