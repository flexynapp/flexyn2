What the audit established

01

Nothing changed. The absence was confirmed, not assumed.

No filter control exists in ProfileTrophies or anywhere that renders it. The component holds no state, so there is nothing a control could drive.

02

"Absent" is the smaller half of this finding.

The locked frames are capped at ten, identical, aria-hidden, and missing entirely at zero earned. A filter cannot be added to a set that carries no identity — that is a different and larger piece of work than a segmented control.

03

The chase list already exists, on the other surface.

The Achievements vault’s "In progress" tab renders every unearned ladder with its live progress bar. That is the real answer to "what am I missing", and it is one tap away and better.

04

So two surfaces would answer the same question, and only one can.

Building a Locked filter here does not add a capability — it adds a second, worse answer beside a good one. That is a finding in itself, and it is the reason the recommendation is negative.

05

The in-repo precedent argues against transferring itself.

CollectionModal.jsx ships All/Owned/Missing over 113 loot items and explains why Missing matters. The reasoning depends on missing items being browsable. Trophies fail that precondition by construction.

06

The zero-earned case is the one that should change.

A brand-new account gets an empty-state card and no frames — so the app shows the shape of the collection to everyone except the person who has none of it. That is one branch at :140.

07

Recommendation: do not build the filter. Make the padlocks a door.

The row of frames is already the visual promise of "there is more" and it currently goes nowhere. Linking it to the vault’s In-progress tab is a few lines, removes the duplicate rather than adding one, and gives the promise somewhere to land.

08

If a filter is built anyway, it needs its own zero case.

A control that can empty the grid needs an empty state distinct from "you have not earned anything yet" — otherwise filtering to Locked on a completed catalog reads as data loss.
