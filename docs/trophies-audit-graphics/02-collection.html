What the audit established

01

Nothing changed. Every figure here was rendered or queried.

A Vite harness mounts ProfileTrophies at 390 px against six fixtures, including sean’s real production rows in the order PostgREST returns them.

02

The set is correct, and that is worth stating plainly.

All 24 ids held in production resolve through getTrophy(). earnedIds is a Set, so nothing can double. The grid and the vault read the same rows from the same listEarned().

03

Defect — the header disagrees with the vault, and this was already reported.

The Achievements audit filed it as D1 on 2026-08-12 and recommended a one-line fix. Re-verified today at 4ff63aa7: the fix has not landed. It is carried here rather than counted twice.

04

Defect — an unresolvable id counts in the numerator and draws no tile.

Rendered: header 6 / 120 above five tiles. earnedIds is built from every row at :41; the grid drops unresolvable ones at :152. The two disagree by construction.

05

Defect — the numerator has no ceiling, and the clock is running.

Both an infinite-tail rung (sessions_x2) and a league season trophy render, count, and sit outside TROPHIES by design. No season has resolved yet, but league_seasons and two crons are live on 28-day seasons — the first resolution puts a row on every participant.

06

Defect — grid-cols-5 on a collection whose count comes from data.

Measured 5+5+1 at one trophy and 5+5+5+5+5+5+1 at twenty-one: a single tile hard against the left edge with four dead columns beside it. tileRow() exists for exactly this, and no col-span-* is present, so the conversion is clean.

07

Defect — a brand-new account sees no locked frames at all.

At zero earned, the empty-state branch at :140 returns the card instead of the grid. The frames exist to show the shape of what is left, and the one person who is all "what is left" never sees them.

08

On a phone, a trophy has no name.

A tile is an emoji, a 2 px tier stripe, a title and an sr-only string. title does not open on touch, and this app ships to iOS and Android only. 🌟 Gold and 💫 Platinum sit side by side distinguished by two colours of hairline.

09

Recommendation: the first three go together as one edit.

Apply the vault’s filter and build earnedIds from rows that resolve. That closes the disagreement, the junk-id inflation and the missing ceiling at once. The grid conversion is separate and independently safe.
